03 · Security

Your data, under lock and key.

A report with your sales and prices is sensitive information. That is why every dashboard we deliver is private:

  • Username and password per client and, if you want, per team member. No access, nothing to see.
  • Access granted and revoked on the spot: if someone leaves the team, their access ends that same day.
  • Encrypted connection (HTTPS) and sessions that expire on their own.
  • Read-only permissions on your accounts: the report looks, it never touches or changes anything.
  • Kept out of search engines: your reports never show up on Google and cannot be shared by mistake through a link.
  • Passwords never travel by email or chat, and we recommend turning on two-step verification in your marketplace and ad accounts.

Frequently asked questions

Which sales channels can be automated?

Marketplaces like Mercado Libre, online stores (VTEX, Tiendanube, Shopify and others), stock and pricing integrators, and ad platforms like Google Ads and Meta Ads. If your channel has data, we can add it to the report.

How often is the report updated?

We agree on it with you: usually daily, and several times a day for prices or stock. You log in and everything is up to date, without asking anyone for anything.

Who can see my data?

Only the people you authorize. Each client gets its own username and password, the connection is encrypted and reports are never indexed by search engines.

Do I need to change my current tools?

No. We work on top of what you already use. We handle setup and maintenance, and you get a report ready to act on.
