Case · Sliabh

Launch of a 3D web experience to discover Argentina's national parks

+367 Active users · week 1
3.3k Events tracked
2k Page views · first 7 days
+600 Scroll events
+2min Average reading time
100% User stickiness (DAU/MAU)

Sliabh is an immersive web experience with 3D-rendered trails to explore Argentina's national parks. La Vicuña designed the site, the tracking setup and the launch strategy: in its first week it reached over 367 active users and 3.3k tracked events, with strong interest from Buenos Aires (CABA and GBA), Rosario, Santa Fe, Córdoba and Mendoza, alongside audiences in U.S. cities (Savannah, New York, Boardman, Prineville, Dallas, among others) and European cities (Stockholm, London, Madrid, Berlin, Rome, Dublin, Oslo, among others). 100% user stickiness confirms that visitors kept coming back, and the project landed its first sponsors within the first month.

Case · T-L Irrigation with HMSA

A project to source potential B2B leads

+300 Contacts
1.6M Impressions
+5k URL clicks
+25k Website sessions
+10k Unique users
Argentina Geo

HMSA, a civil engineering firm and distributor for brands such as T-L, RainBird and GRAF, partnered with La Vicuña on a 360° marketing plan aimed at growers looking into centre-pivot irrigation. The strategy paired qualified lead generation with one-to-one follow-up, sharing information on the safety, warranty and performance of the equipment. With full support from La Vicuña, HMSA closed contracts with the leads it generated.
